Privacy

This starter version stores profile, resume, saved-job and application information in the browser’s local storage on the device being used.

Information is not synchronized to a server in this starter version. Clearing browser data may remove locally saved information.

Before public launch, a complete privacy policy should describe the production hosting, account, analytics, job-listing and data-handling practices.
