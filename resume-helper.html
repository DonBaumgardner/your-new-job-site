Resume Helper

Choose an area to get polished, professional prompts you can adapt honestly to your experience.

Professional Summary

Lead with your role, years or level of experience, strongest skills, and the value you bring.

Template: “[Role] with experience in [area]. Skilled in [skills], with a record of [result]. Ready to contribute [value].”

Work Experience

Start bullets with action verbs, explain what you did, and include a truthful result when possible.

Example structure: “Improved [process] by [action], resulting in [measurable outcome].”

Skills

Prioritize skills required by the job. Use exact, truthful names for software, equipment, certifications and job functions.
